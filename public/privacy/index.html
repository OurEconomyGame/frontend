Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

We store account credentials (username and hashed secrets) to authenticate sessions. We never sell or monetize user data.

Local Storage: Browser localStorage is used for session tokens (oe_token), username (oe_username), user ID (oe_user_id), and UI theme (oe_theme). No tracking cookies are used.

2. Server Logging & Simulation Telemetry

Infrastructure Logs: Technical request metadata (IP addresses, request headers, timestamps) are logged automatically for routing, diagnostics, and security.

Public Ledger: In-game actions (founding companies, trades, orderbook depth, dividends, shifts) are recorded openly on the public simulation ledger.

3. Data Erasure & Succession Transfer

Account Deletion: You may delete your account at any time via your Portfolio settings, purging your credentials from the active database (external CDN logs rotate on standard retention).

Succession Transfer: The database will never be sold. If maintainer succession occurs, data may only transfer to a successor administrator upon approval by 60% of active players, or be purged. See the Backend Privacy Policy on GitHub.

4. Age Policy (13+) & Revisions

OurEconomy is strictly for users aged 13 and older (COPPA/GDPR compliance).

Revisions are published directly to this page. For privacy inquiries, visit Contact Admin or the GitHub Organization.
