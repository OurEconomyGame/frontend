Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

We collect basic account credentials (username and hashed secrets) to authenticate sessions. We do not sell or monetize personal data.

Local Storage: We use browser localStorage to store session tokens (oe_token), your handle (oe_username), user ID (oe_user_id), and UI color theme (oe_theme). No tracking cookies are used.

2. Server Logging & Simulation Telemetry

Infrastructure Logs: Web requests are processed via Google Firebase Hosting and our Bun server, which automatically log technical metadata (IP addresses, request headers, timestamps) for routing, performance, and abuse prevention.

Public Ledger: In-game actions (founding companies, trades, orderbook depth, dividends, and shift records) are recorded openly on the public simulation ledger.

3. Account Deletion & Data Erasure

Upon request, we will delete all user record data and authentication credentials under our direct database control. Note that we cannot modify or purge external logs maintained by third-party infrastructure (such as Google Firebase CDN logs or network transit archives).

4. Age Policy (13+)

OurEconomy is intended solely for users aged 13 and older. Under laws such as COPPA and GDPR, we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 13 without parental consent.

5. Third-Party Links & Policy Revisions

External Links: This site contains links to third-party services (such as GitHub). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Revisions: We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated timestamp. For inquiries, visit the OurEconomyGame Organization.
