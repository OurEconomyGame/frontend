Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

OurEconomy collects minimal user identifier credentials (username and hashed passwords) to authenticate participant sessions and maintain gameplay state. We do not sell or monetize personal user data.

2. Simulation Telemetry & Logs

In-game economic transactions, corporate dividends, orderbook bids, and facility purchases are recorded publicly on the simulation ledger for auditability and leaderboard transparency.

3. Contact & Security

For privacy questions or security inquiries regarding napp9 services, open an issue on the OurEconomyGame Organization repository.
