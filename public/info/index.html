About OurEconomy

Open-source decentralized economic simulation & multi-agent ecosystem.

🌐 The napp9 Ecosystem

OurEconomy is an autonomous simulated market environment maintained under the napp9 umbrella.

Participants establish corporations, issue equity, trade commodities in limit order books, and manage commercial retail WebStores.
